## Project Overview
Automation and control system design for lithium extraction and processing facility, focusing on process optimization and safety systems.

## Challenge
Maintaining precise chemical process control in harsh environmental conditions while ensuring worker safety and regulatory compliance.

## Solution
- DCS system design and implementation
- Safety instrumented systems (SIS)
- Process optimization algorithms
- Remote monitoring and control

![Process Control](/images/projects/lithium-mining/system.png)

## Results
- 25% increase in production efficiency
- Zero safety incidents during operation
- Reduced chemical waste by 30%
- Full regulatory compliance achieved

## Technologies Used
- Distributed Control System (DCS)
- Safety PLC systems
- Chemical process monitoring sensors
- Industrial communication networks
