Kluet Wind Farm – Engineering Design & CAD Services

---

## Project Overview

Tollhurst Engineering provided engineering design and CAD support for the **Kluet Wind Farm**, a utility-scale onshore wind project located in Aceh, Indonesia, developed by UPC Renewables in partnership with local stakeholders and PT PLN.

The engagement focused on delivering contractor-ready design packages to support procurement, tendering, and early works planning. Tollhurst acted as a specialist engineering partner, delivering high-quality technical documentation aligned with international standards and Indonesian project requirements.

---

## Project Details

**Location:** Aceh, Indonesia
**Client/Developer:** UPC Renewables & PT PLN
**Project Type:** Utility-Scale Onshore Wind Farm
**Installed Capacity:** ~72 MW
**Configuration:** 30 × 2.4 MW Wind Turbine Generators
**Engagement Type:** Engineering Design & CAD (Pre-Construction)

---

## Project Context

The Kluet Wind Farm site features challenging terrain with ridges and valleys, requiring careful planning for turbine placement, access roads, and cable routing. The project aims to expand renewable energy capacity in Indonesia and demonstrates the viability of large-scale wind projects in the region. Early-stage engineering was critical to enable efficient procurement and construction.

---

## Tollhurst Scope of Works

### Engineering & CAD Services

Tollhurst’s scope included:

* Turbine layout and set-out drawings
* Foundation conceptual design and general arrangements
* Crane hardstand and access road design
* Cable routing and trench clearance planning
* Mechanical interface detailing at turbine foundations
* Multi-discipline design coordination and clash resolution

All work was delivered in standard CAD formats suitable for contractor use and integration with EPC design packages.

---

## Technical Delivery

### Site Layout & Optimisation

Tollhurst analysed terrain models, wind data, and geotechnical reports to optimise turbine locations for maximum energy yield and constructability.

Deliverables included:

* Turbine coordinate plans
* Constraint and exclusion zone overlays
* Site set-out drawings

### Foundations & Hardstands

Foundations and crane pads were designed parametrically for early contractor pricing and site adaptability.

Deliverables included:

### Access Roads & Crane Pads

Access roads and crane pads were designed to accommodate turbine logistics, minimizing earthworks.

Deliverables included:

* Road alignment drawings
* Longitudinal and cross-section profiles
* Crane pad layout and load envelopes

### Cable Routing & Interfaces

Tollhurst coordinated underground cable routes with foundations, roads, and drainage to prevent clashes and ensure maintainability.

Deliverables included:

* Typical trench detail drawings
