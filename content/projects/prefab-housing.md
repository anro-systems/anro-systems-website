## Project Overview
Engineering design for modular prefabricated housing systems, including structural design, MEP integration, and manufacturing process optimization.

## Challenge
Designing systems for efficient factory production while ensuring on-site assembly simplicity and structural integrity.

## Solution
- 3D BIM modeling of modular units
- MEP systems integration design
- Manufacturing process optimization
- Quality control documentation

## Results
- 40% reduction in on-site assembly time
- Improved manufacturing efficiency by 25%
- Standardized design documentation
- Reduced material waste by 18%

- Revit BIM modeling
- AutoCAD for detailed drawings
- Project management software
- Quality management systems
