## Management

ANRO Systems is managed with a focus on clear accountability, operational rigour, and client outcomes. This page outlines how the company is organised, how decisions are made, and the principles that guide our management approach.

### Overview
We operate with a small executive management team responsible for strategy, operations, finance, and technical delivery. The team sets company-wide priorities, allocates resources, and ensures alignment between client commitments and internal capability.

### Governance & Structure
- Executive management team (CEO, CTO, Operations Director, Finance/Commercial lead)
- Monthly management meetings to review strategy, project status, and financial performance
- Board / advisors provide oversight on major strategic, legal and financial decisions

### Decision-making & Operations
- Strategic decisions are driven by the executive team with input from functional leads
- Project delivery decisions are delegated to project managers with clear escalation paths
- Regular operational reviews (safety, quality, schedule, budget) ensure consistency across projects

### Quality, Safety & Risk
- We maintain documented quality and safety practices applied across projects
- Risk is identified early, tracked, and mitigated through regular reviews and controls
- Continuous improvement is driven by post-project reviews and lessons-learned sessions

### People & Culture
- We prioritise technical excellence, accountability, and collaboration
- Professional development and knowledge sharing are core to how we grow the team
- We aim for transparency in communication and decision-making

### Client Engagement & Accountability
- Single-point contacts for projects with regular progress updates
- Clear scope, milestones, and acceptance criteria agreed before delivery
- Client feedback is actively collected and used to improve services
